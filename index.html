ABOUT US

Building Leaders Who Create Lasting Impact

LYZOA TECHNOLOGY SYSTEMS LTD (RC 1484058) is a Nigerian-registered company incorporated on 4th April 2018 in Wuye, Abuja FCT.

Our specialized operational arm is LTSL — Leadership Training & Strategic Leadership Services, a leadership development and human capacity building initiative.

LTSL is dedicated to empowering leaders, transforming organisations, and building institutions through practical, interactive and results-driven leadership development programmes for both public and private sector organisations.

Our facilitation methodology is case-study driven, participatory, and tailored to the realities of the Nigerian governance and business environment.
